Features

Everything a small store needs to stay stocked

No spreadsheets, no guesswork — just a live picture of your inventory.

▣

Live Inventory Dashboard

See total products, healthy vs. low stock, and total inventory value at a glance — updated the moment anything changes.

📈

Demand Forecasting

A moving average of your recent sales predicts daily demand per product, so restocking decisions are based on data, not guesswork.

⚠

Automatic Reorder Alerts

Reorder point = demand × lead time + safety stock. The moment stock drops below it, that product shows up on your alerts page.

💰

Sales & Profit Tracking

Record every sale in seconds, then see total units sold, revenue, and profit — plus which products are your top sellers.

▤

Custom Categories

Define your own product categories and filter your entire catalog by category with one click.

📱

Private, Per-Account Data

Every account's products, sales, and categories are kept completely separate — your data is yours alone.
